About Me

I am currently a research scientist at Google DeepMind, working on Gemini post-training for agentic coding. Previously, I completed my PhD in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin in 2022. I was advised by Prof. Raymond Mooney and Prof. Junyi Jessy Li. My thesis work focused on building AI models for streamlining software engineering. I was a recipient of the 2020-2021 Bloomberg Data Science Ph.D. Fellowship. Before that, I completed my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign in 2016.
